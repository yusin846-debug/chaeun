# About: source and claim boundaries

Reviewed 2026-09-29. About presents CHAEUN as a modern saju consultant agent linking self-reflection, feng shui interpretation, and personalized interiors/art/object curation.

## Sources
- HSBC / Norman Foster: https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/hsbc-news-archive/back-to-the-future — architect describes consulting a feng shui expert and following advice for escalator axis. Evidence of design practice, not efficacy. No claim that Western architects generally endorse feng shui.
- Designplus, 2025-01-02: https://design.co.kr/article/104073/ — direct interview with Yoo Hyun-joon about architecture as relationships, outdoor access at schools and social design. Not a feng shui endorsement. A verified interview was used instead of an unverified YouTube clip.
- Boubekri et al., 2014: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4031400/ — case-control pilot, 49 office workers, actigraphy subset 21. Association between window/daylight exposure and sleep/quality of life. No causal claim or promised personal outcome.
- Ulrich, 1984: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6143402/ — retrospective comparison of 23 matched pairs of surgical patients, natural vs brick-wall views; shorter stays and less potent analgesic use in nature-view group. Hospital-specific observational findings, not general proof about homes or feng shui.

Environmental studies do not validate saju, five-element prescriptions, luck, wealth, or romantic outcomes. The page explicitly distinguishes empirical environment research from traditional interpretation. Media links are external independent sources, not partners or endorsers.
